BACKGROUND & FOCUS

About Me

"I enjoy the space where physical hardware meets modern software — understanding how signals behave in the real world and writing code to make systems intelligent."

I graduated in 2025 with a B.Tech in Electronics & Communication Engineering from National Institute of Technology Calicut. My time in college gave me a strong grounding in core electrical fundamentals — from analyzing circuit response on breadboards to simulating wave propagation in high-frequency regimes.

For my major project, I worked on computational electromagnetics in CST Studio Suite, designing a reconfigurable graphene antenna in the terahertz band. By tuning the material's chemical potential through electrostatic gating, the array achieves electronic beam-steering and high isolation without any moving physical parts.

Alongside hardware, I've spent considerable time with Google's Agent Development Kit (ADK) and Python, building multi-agent systems where individual agents handle planning, tool execution, and validation to solve complex workflows.

Tunable Terahertz MIMO Antenna

Designed and simulated a graphene ring antenna in the terahertz frequency band using CST Studio Suite. By tuning the chemical potential through electrostatic gating, the antenna achieves dynamic beam steering and >20 dB port isolation in a 2-element MIMO configuration — without requiring physical moving parts.
